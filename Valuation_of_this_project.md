# Project Valuation & Pricing Strategy

## 💰 Recommended Pricing Strategy

Since the software runs **locally** (saving the user monthly cloud fees) and includes **privacy** + **hardware security**, it commands a premium price compared to basic wrappers.

### Option 1: Tiered Pricing (Recommended)

| Tier | Price (USD) | Price (INR) | What they get |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Edition** | **$29 - $39** | **₹2,500** | Unlimited Transcription, All Models, Export to Word/TXT/SRT. |
| **Pro Edition** | **$59 - $79** | **₹5,000** | Everything in Standard + **Audio Preprocessing Suite** + Priority Support. |

### Option 2: All-in-One Lifetime License
*   **Price**: **$49 - $69 (₹4,000 - ₹6,000)**
*   **Strategy**: One price, everything included. "One Payment, Forever."

---

## 🎛️ Feature Valuation

### 1. Base Software (Transcription)
*   **Value**: ~$30 (Comparable to tools like MacWhisper).
*   **Selling Point**: "Stop paying $20/month. Pay once, transcribe forever on your own GPU."
*   **Privacy**: "Your data never leaves your PC." Huge for legal/medical use.

### 2. Audio Preprocessing (The "Pro" Feature)
*   **Add-on Value**: **+$20 to +$30**
*   **Why?**: Replaces tools like Adobe Audition/Audacity for average users.
    *   **Noise Reduction**: Saves hours of cleaning bad audio.
    *   **Music Removal**: Essential for YouTubers/Podcasters.
    *   **VAD/Silence Trimming**: Drastically reduces review time.
    *   **Normalization**: Makes quiet meetings audible.

---

## 💡 Selling Tips
1.  **"No Subscriptions"**: Emphasize this strongly.
2.  **Target Niches**:
    *   **Legal/Medical**: Emphasize **Security/Privacy**.
    *   **Podcasters**: Emphasize **Preprocessing**.
    *   **Students**: Emphasize **Unlimited usage**.
3.  **Distribution**: Consider Gumroad or LemonSqueezy for handling payments and license delivery.
